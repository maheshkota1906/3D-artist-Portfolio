About Me

I’m Durga Naga Mahesh, a passionate 3D artist with three years of hands-on experience creating characters, props, and environments for games and animations. I use Autodesk Maya for 3D modeling and Photoshop for texturing, with a focus on creating both stylized and realistic assets for real-time applications.

My work focuses on clean topology, UV mapping, texture baking, and game-ready asset production. I enjoy creating detailed characters, props, and environments that support gameplay and cinematic experiences.

I’m focused on developing high-quality 3D assets with strong attention to detail, visual quality, and efficient real-time workflows.
